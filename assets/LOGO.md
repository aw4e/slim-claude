# slim-claude logo

One stem splitting into three tines: one request delegated to specialists. Built from round-capped strokes
on a 256 grid (stroke 26; the small cut uses 36).

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo.svg` | Master, teal on light backgrounds |
| `logo-white.svg` | White, for dark backgrounds |
| `logo-small.svg` | Heavier cut for 32 px and below (favicons, tiny list icons) |
| `icon.svg` | App-icon tile (mint on ink). The 1024 px PNG is `.claude-plugin/icon.png` |

**Colour**
- Teal `#0F766E` on white: 5.5:1 contrast.
- Mint `#5EEAD4` on ink `#0B1220`: 12.7:1. Tile and dark backgrounds use ink `#0B1220`.
- One-colour use is always fine (black or white).

**Do**
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least the width of one tine.
- Use `logo-small.svg` at 32 px and below. Never go under 16 px.

**Don't**
- Stretch, rotate, outline, add effects, or recolour outside the palette above.
- Use it to imply endorsement by Anthropic or by the projects credited in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

**Known limits**
- The SVGs are stroke-based (not expanded to outlines); they render correctly everywhere SVG strokes are supported.
- No trademark search was done. The mark resembles a generic branching or trident symbol; check before commercial use.
